Privacy Policy

xhs-to-tiktok is a personal automation tool used only by its owner to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account (@darcyyoyoyoyo).

Data collection

The tool does not collect, store, or share any personal data of any users. It operates only on the account owner's own content and credentials, which are stored locally on the owner's computer.

Third parties

The tool communicates only with official platform APIs (TikTok Open API and the source platform that hosts the owner's own videos). No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions, contact the account owner via TikTok @darcyyoyoyoyo.
